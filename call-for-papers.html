10th Conference on Fixed Income Markets

Fixed Income Research and Implications for Monetary Policy

29–30 April 2027 · Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago · Chicago, United States

The Bank of Canada, the Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago and the Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco are hosting a conference on fixed income research and implications for monetary policy. The conference will provide an opportunity for researchers to discuss theoretical and empirical issues in fixed income research and how they relate to the conduct of monetary policy.

Call for papers

Topics of particular interest include

  • Macroeconomic determinants of yields, including inflation, long-run growth and productivity
  • Fiscal policy, government debt supply and debt management
  • International fixed-income markets, exchange rates and global demand for safe assets
  • Intermediation frictions and market structure, including in repo and funding markets
  • Monetary-policy implementation and transmission

We will also consider papers on fixed income research broadly defined, such as term structure modeling, bond market volatility, estimation of risk premia, default risk, corporate bond markets and securitization.

Submit a paper

Send a draft paper or a detailed abstract to chi.fixed.income.markets.conference@chi.frb.org by 15 December 2026.

Authors of accepted papers will be notified by mid-January. The organizers will cover reasonable travel expenses for presenters. Electronic copies of the conference papers will be made available online.
