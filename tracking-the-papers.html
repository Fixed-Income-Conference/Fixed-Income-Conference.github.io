Tracking the papers

Publication audit as of 25 August 2026.

Of the 113 research papers presented across the nine numbered editions, 63 have appeared in peer-reviewed journals and one more is forthcoming. Keynotes, policy talks, panels and discussions are excluded from the count. Daniel Urban’s interactive conference ranking has interesting information on publication rates across finance conferences.

One thing this chart does not say. An impact factor belongs to a journal, not to a conference: these bars record where papers presented here eventually appeared, and nothing more. They are not a measure of the impact of the conference or of the series, and none is claimed.
